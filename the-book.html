A Novel by Rabi Samuel

Cold Mine

Some secrets were never meant to be unearthed.

Beneath the poisoned hills of Kolar Gold Fields, India, lies a Chola treasury that has slept for a thousand years. Sealed by ancient priests. Buried again by colonial hands. Guarded by a goddess the miners never stopped praying to.

Heritage researcher Rahul Kumar and photojournalist Rani Menon uncover it. So does someone else.

A man from the same streets has spent thirty years preparing for this moment. He is not chasing treasure, but what the British buried before they left. He believes it was stolen. He intends to take it back. Whatever it costs. He is not entirely wrong.

And in the depths of the mine, something has been waiting for all of them.

A thriller of forbidden heritage, colonial betrayal, and a goddess who never forgets.
